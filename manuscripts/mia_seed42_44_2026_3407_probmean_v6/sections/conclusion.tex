\section{Conclusion}
Across training seeds 42, 44, 2026, and 3407, forecast-attention readout had the highest mean AUROC and AP among all four variants and the highest mean Macro F1 among the principal variants. The supplementary mean-pooling control had similar mean Macro AUROC and higher F1. The complete-versus-\nojepa{} Macro AUROC difference met the nine-test principal-family criterion but not the all-90 correction; lower specificity than auxiliary-supervised direct readout remained detectable under both secondary-family and all-90 correction. Probability-quality and hypothetical-utility summaries showed further trade-offs. These exploratory results motivate independent validation, complete upstream provenance, stronger matched baselines, and prospectively fixed training and statistical policies.
