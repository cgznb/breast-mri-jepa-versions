\section{Conclusion}
Using seed 44 and evaluating five fold models separately, forecast-attention readout had the highest mean Macro AUROC and AP, while auxiliary-supervised direct readout had the highest mean Macro F1. None of the nine principal comparisons met its adjusted significance criterion. Forecast attention's greater sensitivity accompanied lower specificity, and several operating-point contrasts remained detectable after correction. Probability-quality, subgroup, and hypothetical-utility analyses further qualified the discrimination means. These exploratory results motivate independent validation, complete upstream provenance, stronger matched baselines, and prospectively fixed training and statistical policies.
