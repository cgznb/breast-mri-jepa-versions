\section{Conclusion}
Across training seeds 44, 2026, and 3407, forecast-attention readout had the highest mean Macro AUROC and AP among all four variants and the highest mean Macro F1 among the principal variants. The supplementary mean-pooling control had similar mean Macro AUROC and an F1 point estimate higher by only 0.00032. No principal AUROC/AP/F1 comparison passed nine-test Holm adjustment. Lower specificity than auxiliary-supervised direct readout remained detectable under both secondary-family and all-90 correction; probability quality and hypothetical utility showed further trade-offs. These exploratory results require independent validation, complete upstream provenance, stronger matched baselines, and prospectively fixed training and statistical policies.
